## 1. Permitted Use

The publicly released IMNU-Yearbook dataset is provided solely for
academic and scientific research, education related to such research,
and reproducibility of scholarly results.

Users may:

- download and use the released dataset for scientific research;
- conduct retrieval, NLP, data analysis, benchmarking, and related
  non-production research experiments;
- create and publish research results, statistics, models, figures,
  tables, and other derived research outputs;
- cite and describe the dataset in scholarly publications.

Viewing, downloading, cloning, and forking the official public GitHub
repository for permitted academic and scientific research use are
allowed subject to these terms.

## 2. Attribution

Any publication or public research output using this dataset must
provide appropriate attribution to the IMNU-Yearbook dataset and,
when applicable, cite the associated paper.

Citation metadata are provided in CITATION.cff.

## 3. Restrictions

Unless separate written permission is obtained, users may not:

- use the dataset for commercial products or commercial services;
- sell, sublicense, or commercially redistribute the dataset;
- independently republish or redistribute the corpus as a separate
  dataset or substantially equivalent corpus collection outside the
  normal functionality of the official repository;
- remove or obscure attribution or these use terms;
- attempt to reverse anonymization or identify anonymized institutional
  information;
- use the dataset to profile, investigate, or make decisions about
  identifiable individuals;
- claim that the dataset represents all universities or all university
  yearbooks.

Researchers who wish to share experiments may link to, clone, or fork
the official repository under these terms.

## 4. Derived Research Outputs

These terms do not restrict publication of aggregate statistics,
evaluation results, trained model parameters, research code, or other
derived research outputs that do not reproduce substantial portions
of the corpus text.

Short excerpts may be used when reasonably necessary for scholarly
discussion, subject to applicable law and appropriate attribution.

## 5. Source Material and Rights

The public release contains an anonymized and processed research
dataset derived from university yearbook materials.

The release does not include the original yearbook PDF files.

These terms apply only to rights that the dataset maintainers are
authorized to grant. They do not override copyright, privacy,
institutional, or other rights that may exist in underlying source
materials.

## 6. Privacy and Responsible Use

The public corpus has undergone anonymization and sensitive-information
review. This process does not imply a guarantee of complete anonymity
or zero re-identification risk.

Users must not intentionally attempt to re-identify anonymized entities
or combine the dataset with external information for identification.

The dataset is provided "as is", without warranties of accuracy,
completeness, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement,
